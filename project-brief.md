# Project brief

## The question
Which wards in Ibadan North are furthest from a health facility, and how many people live in them?

## Why it matters
Primary health centres are where most families in Ibadan North go first. A ward with many people and no facility nearby means long walks for antenatal visits, immunisation and emergencies. Knowing which wards those are helps a local government decide where the next facility should go.

## The data I need
1. Ward boundaries for Ibadan North LGA.
2. Population per ward.
3. Locations of health facilities, with their type.

## What a good answer looks like
A map of the wards shaded by population, with every facility marked, and a short list of the wards that have people but no facility inside them.
